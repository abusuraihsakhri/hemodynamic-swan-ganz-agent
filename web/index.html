Equations and units

Cardiac index = CO / BSA.

SVR = 80 × (MAP − RAP) / CO, in dyn·s/cm⁵.

PVR = 80 × (mPAP − PCWP) / CO, in dyn·s/cm⁵; divide by 80 for Wood units.

Transpulmonary gradient = mPAP − PCWP, in mmHg.

Direct Fick CO = VO₂ / [10 × (CaO₂ − CvO₂)], in L/min. Oxygen contents are in mL/dL and VO₂ in mL/min.

The displayed CO input remains the denominator for CI/SVR/PVR. Optional Fick CO is displayed separately and is not silently substituted.
